# Real Photo Asset Checklist — Owner to Provide / Approve

The generated concept board is a visual reference only. These are the authentic photo assets that will make the final website credible.

## Priority 1 — Hero and identity
- [ ] Official company logo (SVG or high-resolution transparent PNG)
- [ ] Wide horizontal photo of actual glass processing line/factory (original file)
- [ ] Wide photo of real showroom / office / facility
- [ ] Confirmed company address and approved public contact details

## Priority 2 — Product photography
- [ ] Toughened glass sheets, edges or finished product
- [ ] Mirrors / LED mirrors
- [ ] Slim partitions
- [ ] Decorative glass samples
- [ ] Railing glass or shower glass if actually supplied
- [ ] Close-ups showing finish and detail

## Priority 3 — Projects and people
- [ ] 5–12 actual completed projects with permission to publish
- [ ] Before/after or process images where available
- [ ] Staff/team or manufacturing process photos, with consent
- [ ] Captions: project type, city/area, product used, completion date if approved
- [ ] Permission to show customer names, property names or logos (otherwise omit)

## For each image, record
- File name
- Who took/owns it
- Written permission status
- What it shows
- Whether it is actual company work
- Alt text
- Any customer/location details that must be withheld

Do not use screenshots of social media posts as the final image asset if the original file can be obtained. Do not remove watermarks or imply stock/generated images are company work. If you want to reuse images from Instagram/Facebook, download the original only where permitted and confirm you have rights to publish them on the company website.
