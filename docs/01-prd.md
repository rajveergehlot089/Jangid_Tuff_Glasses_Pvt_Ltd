# PRD — Jangid Tuff Glasses Website

**Version:** 1.0 · **Status:** Draft for owner validation  
**Product:** Marketing, portfolio and lead-generation website  
**Primary market:** Jodhpur, Rajasthan; regional B2B and residential project enquiries

## 1. Vision
Create a distinctive, premium, image-led digital presence for Jangid Tuff Glasses Private Limited, positioning the business around glass processing, glass products and modern interior/architectural applications. The website should generate qualified enquiries from homeowners, architects, interior designers, contractors, builders, commercial operators and glass trade buyers.

## 2. Goals
1. Explain the business and its product categories quickly.
2. Showcase genuine products, manufacturing/processing capabilities and completed projects when verified.
3. Convert visitors into phone calls, WhatsApp conversations and structured quote enquiries.
4. Improve local discovery through technically sound SEO and useful product/service landing pages.
5. Make it easy to maintain products, gallery items and FAQs later.
6. Establish a premium identity that is visually differentiated from generic contractor/manufacturer templates.

## 3. Non-goals for MVP
- E-commerce checkout or online payment.
- Automated pricing without a verified price list and pricing rules.
- Claims of certifications, machine capabilities, warranties or service guarantees without evidence.
- Fake testimonials, fake project counts, fake reviews or stock images presented as company work.
- Customer account portal unless later approved.

## 4. Audiences and needs
- **Architects / interior designers:** accurate product information, finish references, project gallery, quick technical conversation.
- **Contractors / builders:** bulk/project enquiries, dimensions, quantities, delivery/installation discussion.
- **Homeowners:** visual inspiration, easy-to-understand product categories, clear contact route.
- **Commercial / hospitality / retail:** project enquiry, coordination and custom requirements.
- **Trade buyers:** product scope, location, phone/WhatsApp contact and response path.

## 5. Core journeys
- Landing page → explore product category → product details → quote form / WhatsApp.
- Landing page → projects/gallery → view project → enquire about a similar solution.
- Search engine → local product landing page → call or quote request.
- Landing page → services → understand process → submit project brief.
- Contact page → phone, directions, WhatsApp or enquiry form.

## 6. Required pages
- Home
- About
- Products index
- Product detail template
- Services index
- Service detail template where content justifies it
- Projects / portfolio
- Gallery
- Contact / Request a quote
- FAQ
- Privacy policy
- Terms
- Custom 404
- Thank-you / form status page

## 7. Product taxonomy
Potential categories requiring owner confirmation:
- Toughened glass
- Clear / float glass
- Mirrors and LED mirrors
- Slim glass partitions
- Decorative glass
- Glass railings
- Shower enclosures
- Custom glass requirements
- Other glass processing options only if the business confirms them.

Each product page should include: summary, typical applications, available variants only if verified, project image, care/selection notes where approved, FAQs, related categories, CTA. Never invent specifications, thickness availability, test results or prices.

## 8. Lead-generation requirements
- Header CTA: “Request a Quote”.
- Sticky mobile call / WhatsApp action that does not cover content.
- Product-specific enquiry buttons.
- Quote form with name, phone, optional email, customer type, product interest, project location, approximate dimensions, quantity, target timeline, notes and optional reference file upload (only if secure upload is implemented).
- WhatsApp fallback with a prefilled message.
- Track CTA clicks and form outcomes after privacy-conscious analytics is approved.
- Clear consent and honest submission status.
- Store enquiries only through a secure backend; no secret keys in client-side code.

## 9. Success metrics
Establish a baseline after launch. Track:
- Qualified enquiries per month.
- Calls and WhatsApp clicks.
- Quote form completion rate.
- Product-page-to-enquiry conversion.
- Organic impressions and clicks for relevant local search terms.
- Core Web Vitals and mobile usability.
Do not fabricate target values before baseline data exists.

## 10. Brand and content principles
- Premium, precise, confident, architectural.
- Real company photography preferred above generic stock.
- Editorial copy, short scannable sections and meaningful CTAs.
- English as primary language; structure content so Hindi can be added later.
- Avoid inflated superlatives and unverified “best”, “No. 1”, “trusted by thousands” claims.

## 11. Business information validation
Before launch, owner must confirm: legal display name, address to publish, contact number, opening hours, service area, product range, manufacturing/processing capabilities, installation support, warranties, certifications, real project permissions, social links and privacy contact.

## 12. MVP acceptance criteria
- All required pages work on mobile and desktop.
- Navigation and CTAs have valid destinations.
- Product and project filters work and are keyboard accessible.
- Forms validate input and show truthful success/error states.
- WhatsApp message uses correctly encoded fields.
- No invented reviews, specs, prices or business achievements.
- Real photo assets have owner permission and accurate alt text.
- Metadata, sitemap, robots and structured data are implemented accurately.
- No TypeScript files or TypeScript syntax.
- No critical console errors; build and lint pass.
